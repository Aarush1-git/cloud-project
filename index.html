How it works

The cloud only ever stores ciphertext. Your passphrase never leaves the browser.

1

Split

The file is cut into 1 MB blocks.

2

Derive key

PBKDF2 (SHA-256, 200k rounds) turns your passphrase into a key.

3

Encrypt

Each block gets AES-256-GCM with a unique IV, bound to its index.

4

Upload

Blocks and a manifest go to S3 via short-lived presigned URLs.

5

Download

Fetched through CloudFront, decrypted, reassembled, and hash-verified.

Architecture

Follow the moving blocks. Upload goes straight to S3; download comes back through the CDN.

Upload: the browser encrypts each block, then PUTs it straight to S3 Download: S3 to CloudFront to browser, which decrypts
Lambda only signs URLs. It never sees your file or your passphrase.
